Expiry Calculation: Start date + 1 year (handles leap years correctly).

Statuses:

  • EXPIRING SOON - 30 days or fewer remaining
  • EXPIRED - License has expired

After Expiry: Only Admin can access the ERP. Counsellors and Accountants are blocked until the license is renewed.
