Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is the admission process free?
Yes! Our initial counselling is 100% free. Our service charges are only applicable when you confirm an admission, and are clearly disclosed upfront.
Which universities do you work with?
We work with 50+ UGC-recognized universities across India including Anna University, SRM, VIT, Amity, SASTRA, Bharathidasan University, and many more.
How soon will I get a response?
Our counsellors typically respond within 30 minutes during business hours. You can also reach us anytime through the admission form.
Do you help with distance education?
Absolutely! We specialize in UGC-approved distance learning programs from top central and state universities across India.
What documents do I need?
Typically: 10th & 12th marksheets, Transfer Certificate, Domicile, passport-size photos, and Aadhaar. We guide you through every document requirement.
Are the universities genuine and approved?
We only work with UGC/AICTE-approved, government-recognised universities. We do not recommend any unapproved or fake institutions.
